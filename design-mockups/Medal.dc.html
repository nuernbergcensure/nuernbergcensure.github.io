The award

The medal

A 66 mm medal in blackened bronze. It recalls the 1946 judgment, not the executions that followed.

Specifications

Diameter66 mm
MetalBronze, blackened (oxidized) finish
FrontJoachim von Ribbentrop in the dock at the Nuremberg trial
BackThe empty defendants' dock of Courtroom 600, with Nemo supra legem
EngravingHonoree's name, year and category

The empty chair

No honoree is expected to collect the medal. Each one is placed on an empty chair in a replica of the dock and held in escrow.

It is handed over only if the honoree appears before a lawful court for the acts cited, and then it becomes part of that court's record.

The medal carries no flags, emblems or symbols of the Nazi regime, and no reference to execution.

Photo sources for the portrait
