# The Nuremberg Prize — Evidence Dossier and Indictment Formats

The formats every award follows. Text in [square brackets] is filled in each year. Every template follows the Wording safeguard (Article 10): state documented findings, attribute each one to its source, and never declare anyone guilty unless a court has convicted them.

**Wording rules for every template**

| Write | Never write |
| --- | --- |
| "The UN Commission of Inquiry found that forces under [Name]'s command…" | "[Name] committed…" |
| "The ICC issued an arrest warrant for [Name] on [date] for…" | "[Name] is guilty of…" or "war criminal [Name]" |
| "According to [source], at least [number] people were killed" | Adjectives the source doesn't use ("monstrous," "evil") |
| "[Name] has denied the allegations" (when they have) | Leaving out a denial that is on record |

## Template A — Evidence dossier

*Prepared by the advisers for every shortlisted candidate (sealed). Only the honoree's dossier is published.*

**Cover**

- Candidate: [full name]
- Position(s) held at the time of the acts: [title, country or organization, dates]
- Category: [Atrocity]
- Cycle: [2027]
- Dossier ID: [NP-2027-01]
- Status: ☐ Sealed draft ☐ Approved for publication
- Prepared by: [adviser(s)] · Last updated: [date]

**1. Summary** *(three sentences at most, every claim attributed)*

[According to [body], [what happened], [where], [when]. [Body] found that [candidate] [role]. [Candidate] has not faced a court for these acts.]

**2. The acts**

For each incident or pattern: what happened, where, when, and the scale. Every sentence ends with a source reference, for example [S1].

**3. The candidate's role and authority**

- Position and powers at the time
- Chain of command or decision-making link to the acts
- Evidence that they ordered the acts, knew of them, or could have stopped them

**4. Formal findings (the evidence gate)**

| # | Finding | Issued by | Date | Reference or link | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | [e.g., arrest warrant] | [ICC Pre-Trial Chamber] | [date] | [link] | [outstanding] |

**Evidence gate:** ☐ Pass (at least one formal finding above) ☐ Fail

**5. Supporting evidence**

Reports from human rights organizations, investigative journalism and survivor testimony. Label each item clearly as supporting, not as a formal finding.

**6. Accountability status (impunity)**

- Any proceedings opened, blocked or stalled, and why
- Immunity claimed or granted
- Whether the candidate is beyond the reach of any court that has jurisdiction

**7. The candidate's position**

- Public denials or explanations already on record, quoted fairly
- *(Honoree only, added after notice)* Summary of their response and link to it in full

**8. What is disputed**

Facts that are contested, credible counterarguments, and any weaknesses in the evidence. A dossier with no disputed points has not been examined hard enough.

**9. Sources**

[S1] [Author or body], [title], [date], [link]. List every source cited.

**10. Review log**

| Step | By | Date | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence gate checked | [adviser] | | |
| Legal review | [lawyer or clinic] | | |
| Approved for publication | [advisers] | | |

## Template B — The Indictment

*The hand-lettered document that accompanies the medal. Keep the citation to one sentence.*

> **THE NUREMBERG PRIZE FOR [ATROCITY]**
> **[2027]**
>
> The Nuremberg Prize Foundation, on the decision of its [advisers / Committee], enters into the public record that
>
> **[FULL NAME]**
> [position held at the time of the acts]
>
> is named the [2027] recipient of the Nuremberg Prize for [Atrocity], on the following finding:
>
> *[One attributed sentence, e.g., "whom the [body] found, on [date], to bear responsibility for [acts] in [place] between [dates]."]*
>
> This record is not a verdict. It stands until [Name] answers to a lawful court.
>
> **Nemo supra legem.**
>
> In recognition of those harmed, the public is invited to support [victims' organization].
>
> Entered at [city], 1 October [2027]
> For the [advisers / Committee]: ______________________
