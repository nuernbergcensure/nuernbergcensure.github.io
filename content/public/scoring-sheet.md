## Step 1 — Evidence gate (pass or fail)

The candidate goes forward only if at least one of these exists and is cited in the dossier:

- [ ] ICC arrest warrant or indictment
- [ ] UN commission of inquiry or fact-finding report
- [ ] Judgment of a national or international court
- [ ] Concurring findings by at least two recognized human rights or environmental organizations

## Step 2 — Scoring (1 = lowest, 5 = highest)

| Factor | Guide | Score (1–5) | Note (one line) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gravity | 1 = limited harm to few people · 3 = serious harm to thousands · 5 = mass harm or death on a national scale | | |
| Responsibility | 1 = remote link · 3 = knew and failed to stop it · 5 = personally ordered or directed it | | |
| Impunity | 1 = already facing proceedings · 3 = proceedings blocked or stalled · 5 = no accountability of any kind | | |
| Recency | 1 = ended more than 10 years ago · 3 = ended within 5 years · 5 = ongoing | | |
| **Total** | Out of 20, all factors weighted equally | | |
